01 · Logo & app icon

The mark

Gold candy lettering on a purple squircle — and the P is a smiling face, mid-yap. The whole idea in one glyph: purple is the stage, gold is the reward, and it’s already talking to you.

  • Y

    Candy wordmark

    “YAP” in chunky, rounded, puffed gold caps — custom lettering (not Fraunces or Nunito). foil-gold face over a soft foil-amber underglow gives the pressable 3D candy feel.

  • ◡

    The smiling P

    The P’s counter is drawn as a mouth, so the letter reads as a face. The mark isn’t just a name — it’s a friendly thing that talks back.

  • ))

    Talk-lines

    Three gold strokes radiating off the P — sound coming out. The literal act of yapping, baked into the logo.

  • ▢

    The squircle field

    A studio-violet rounded square — the brand primary, the “stage” you step onto. Full-bleed purple, no border.

studio-violet · field foil-gold · letters + talk-lines foil-amber · 3D underglow

Do

  • Keep gold letters on the purple field — that pairing is the brand.
  • Keep the lettering rounded, chunky, and puffed.
  • Give the mark clear space; let the talk-lines breathe.
  • Use the squircle as the app icon; scale it as one unit.

Don’t

  • Recolor the letters or set them in a serif (that’s Fraunces’ job in-app, not the logo’s).
  • Outline, emboss, or add a drop-shadow beyond the built-in candy depth.
  • Crowd or clip the talk-lines, or drop the smile from the P.
  • Place the mark on a busy or non-brand background.

Note · supersedes the doc

This locks the app-icon direction that the design doc left open (§12 floated “a gold mic on the spotlight with a Fraunces ‘Y’”). The wordmark is custom rounded candy lettering, not Fraunces — Fraunces still owns every in-app heading and number.

02 · Color

The palette is a discipline

Purple is the room you stand in; gold is the prize you earn. Every value is a token — never hardcode hex in components.

Functional (kept deliberately tiny)

Purple + gold + white only

Coral and mint appear only as rare functional signals — never decoration. If a screen has more than these, cut something.

11 · Voice & UX copy

Warm, plain, a little cheeky

Sentence case. Short. Actions say what happens. Never grade — always nudge. Errors are calm and directive.

Appendix

Gaps — specified, not guessed

Items the design doc references but does not give hard tokens for. Flagged here rather than invented, so no off-brand values slip into the system.

  • Motion easing curves. Durations are tokenized; specific bezier curves are not (doc says only “ease-out”). This sheet uses a pragmatic cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) as a placeholder — needs a motion spec to lock.
  • Yapbot mascot artwork. Explicitly “to be commissioned” (doc §4.10, §12). Repped as a slot; expressions + a confetti pose still to be designed.
  • Logo guidelines. The app icon is now locked (§01, from yap-logo-mockup.png). Still to formalize: a clear-space rule, minimum sizes, a one-color/monochrome variant, and a horizontal wordmark lockup for headers and share cards. The source vector/font for the “YAP” lettering should be archived as a brand asset.
  • Tint opacities. Hairline (lilac 15%), delta-chip backgrounds (mint/coral 15%) are stated as ratios inline, not named tokens. Derived here from the base tokens; consider promoting to real tokens.
  • Spotlight headline scrim. Doc mentions a “subtle radial darken behind headlines over the spotlight” for AA — no value given; needs a defined overlay token.
  • Audio-yap waveform. Described as an animated foil-gold waveform on --grad-violet — visual is directional; amplitude/animation spec pending.
